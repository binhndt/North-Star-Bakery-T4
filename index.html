Welcome to North Star Bakery

Fresh From Our Neighborhood Oven

Good morning, neighbor! North Star Bakery is a handmade, community-centered bakery where everything is mixed, shaped, and baked in small batches each day. Whether you are grabbing breakfast on your commute, picking up treats for the family, or planning an event, we are glad you stopped by.
