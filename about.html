About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began as a small weekend stand at the farmers' market and grew into the neighborhood bakery we are today. We named it for the star that guides travelers home, because we want our bakery to feel like a place you always know how to find.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We bake with simple ingredients and buy from nearby producers whenever we can. Our flour comes from regional mills, our eggs and dairy come from local farms, and our fruit changes with the seasons.
